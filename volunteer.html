Why Volunteer with GBL?

Green Circular Lab welcomes people who want to contribute to environmental sustainability, technology, innovation and community development. Volunteers can share their knowledge, creativity and time to support our mission.

Our Volunteer Opportunities

🌱 Environmental Conservation

Support tree planting awareness, waste management, recycling and environmental education.

💻 ICT and Digital Skills

Support digital literacy, computer skills training and technology awareness activities.

🚀 Youth Entrepreneurship

Help young people develop creativity, business ideas and practical entrepreneurship skills.

📢 Community Outreach

Assist with awareness campaigns, community engagement and educational activities.

📸 Media and Communication

Contribute photography, graphic design, writing, social media and storytelling skills.

🤝 Administration and Research

Support documentation, research, planning, reporting and organisational development.

Who Can Volunteer?

  • University and secondary school students.
  • Teachers and educators.
  • Environmental activists.
  • ICT professionals and technology enthusiasts.
  • Entrepreneurs and business mentors.
  • Community members and other interested individuals.

Volunteers should be willing to learn, cooperate and respect GBL's values.
