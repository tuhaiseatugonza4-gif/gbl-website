1. Why Support GBL?

Green Circular Lab (GBL) is a youth-driven development initiative in Uganda seeking to address environmental, technological, economic and social challenges through innovation, education and community participation.

Your support can help us develop practical programmes that empower young people and communities to participate in sustainable development.

2. Our Priority Areas

Environmental Conservation

Support environmental education, tree planting initiatives, waste reduction and ecosystem protection.

Climate Action

Support climate awareness, environmental resilience education and locally appropriate climate solutions.

ICT and Digital Innovation

Help young people access digital knowledge, technology skills and opportunities for innovation.

Youth Entrepreneurship

Promote creativity, practical business skills, innovation and sustainable livelihood opportunities.

3. How Your Support Can Help

Contributions may support activities such as:

  • Environmental awareness and education materials.
  • Tree planting and community conservation activities.
  • ICT learning materials and digital skills training.
  • Youth entrepreneurship workshops.
  • Community outreach and educational activities.
  • Project monitoring, reporting and administration.

The actual activities supported will depend on available resources, donor agreements and approved project budgets.

4. Ways to Support GBL

Financial Contributions

Individuals and institutions may contact GBL to request verified organisational donation and transfer details.

Equipment and Materials

Support may include computers, ICT equipment, educational materials, environmental tools and other relevant resources.

Technical Expertise

Professionals, researchers and volunteers can contribute knowledge, mentorship and technical assistance.

Institutional Partnerships

Organisations and companies can collaborate with GBL on environmental, technology and entrepreneurship initiatives.

5. Transparency and Accountability

GBL is committed to responsible management of resources received from supporters and development partners.

  • Contributions should be documented appropriately.
  • Funds should be used for their agreed purposes.
  • Financial records should be maintained.
  • Donors should receive appropriate updates and reports.
  • Misuse of organisational resources is prohibited.

GBL promotes integrity, transparency and accountability through its organisational governance and policies.

6. How to Make a Donation

Important donation security notice: Please contact GBL directly to obtain and verify official organisational payment instructions before transferring money. Do not send funds to an unverified personal account or number.
