Our Institutional Policies

Green Circular Lab (GBL) recognises the importance of strong institutional policies in promoting ethical conduct, protecting communities, managing resources responsibly and achieving sustainable development.

01

Anti-Corruption and Anti-Bribery Policy

Promotes honesty, integrity and zero tolerance for bribery, fraud, abuse of office and corruption in organisational activities.

Proposed – Pending approval
02

Financial Management Policy

Establishes standards for budgeting, expenditure, financial records, accountability, procurement and financial reporting.

Proposed – Pending approval
03

Environmental Protection Policy

Encourages environmental responsibility, waste reduction, recycling, climate action and sustainable use of natural resources.

Proposed – Pending approval
04

Child Safeguarding Policy

Sets out the intended principles and procedures for protecting children from abuse, exploitation, neglect and harm during activities.

Proposed – Pending approval
05

Human Resources Policy

Provides a framework for fair recruitment, staff conduct, equal opportunities, performance management and workplace responsibilities.

Proposed – Pending approval
06

Whistleblower Protection Policy

Encourages safe reporting of suspected misconduct, fraud, corruption and unethical practices without retaliation against good-faith reporters.

Proposed – Pending approval
07

Conflict of Interest Policy

Promotes impartial decision-making and disclosure of personal or financial interests that could influence organisational decisions.

Proposed – Pending approval
08

Procurement Policy

Promotes fair supplier selection, transparent purchasing, value for money and responsible procurement practices.

Proposed – Pending approval

Important Institutional Notice

The policies listed on this page represent GBL's proposed institutional policy framework. They should not be interpreted as formally approved policies until they have been reviewed and adopted by the appropriate authorised governing body. Approved documents will be published here with their effective dates and version numbers.

Our Commitment to Accountability

GBL aims to strengthen institutional accountability through:

  • Transparent financial management and reporting.
  • Ethical leadership and responsible decision-making.
  • Protection of children and vulnerable communities.
  • Responsible environmental practices.
  • Fair procurement and resource management.
  • Monitoring, evaluation and organisational learning.
  • Respect for applicable Ugandan laws and regulations.
