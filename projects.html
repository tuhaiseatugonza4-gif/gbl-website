Creating a Greener, Smarter and More Inclusive Future

What We Do

Green Circular Lab (GBL) is committed to promoting sustainable development through environmental conservation, climate action, digital innovation, entrepreneurship and community empowerment.

We seek to work with young people, women, communities, institutions and development partners to create practical solutions to social, environmental and economic challenges in Uganda.

Our Main Programmes

1. Environmental Conservation

Promoting tree planting, biodiversity protection, waste management, recycling and environmental education.

Programme area

2. Climate Action

Supporting climate education, environmental awareness, climate resilience and community participation in climate solutions.

Programme area

3. ICT and Digital Innovation

Encouraging computer literacy, digital skills, coding, technology education and creative problem-solving.

Programme area

4. Youth Entrepreneurship

Developing business skills, creativity, innovation, enterprise development and sustainable livelihood opportunities.

Programme area

5. Community Empowerment

Promoting inclusive participation, education, practical skills and opportunities for young people, women and vulnerable communities.
