OUR IMPACT AND COMMITMENT

Building a greener, smarter and more inclusive future through environmental conservation, technology, innovation and entrepreneurship.

1. Our Commitment to Sustainable Development

Green Circular Lab (GBL) is a youth-driven development initiative in Uganda working towards practical solutions to environmental, technological, economic and social challenges.

Our impact approach focuses on empowering communities, encouraging innovation and supporting sustainable development through education, partnerships and action.

Transparency statement: GBL is developing its programmes and impact measurement framework. The activities described on this page are priority areas and planned initiatives, not claims of completed results.

4. How We Intend to Measure Impact

GBL aims to use measurable indicators to assess programme performance and communicate results responsibly.

  • Number of people participating in programme activities.
  • Number of environmental education activities conducted.
  • Number of trees planted and their survival rates, where applicable.
  • Number of young people participating in digital skills training.
  • Number of youth enterprise initiatives supported.
  • Community feedback and evidence of positive change.

Results will only be published when they are supported by reliable records and appropriate evidence.
