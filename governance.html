Governance & Leadership

Integrity in Action. Transparency in Leadership. Sustainability in Development.

Our Governance Approach

Green Circular Lab (GBL) is committed to responsible leadership, ethical conduct, transparency and accountability. Our governance approach is intended to ensure that organisational decisions, resources and programmes serve our mission and the communities we seek to support.

Leadership and Management

Founder and Executive Leadership

Provides strategic direction, promotes the mission and coordinates organisational development.

Programme Management

Coordinates programme planning, implementation, monitoring and reporting.

Administration and Operations

Supports records management, communication, logistics and day-to-day operations.

Proposed Organisational Structure

1. Board of Directors / Governing Body
Provides strategic oversight and governance.

2. Executive Leadership
Provides organisational leadership and implements approved strategy.

3. Programme and Project Teams
Coordinate environment, ICT, innovation and entrepreneurship activities.

4. Finance and Administration
Supports financial management, documentation and administration.

5. Community Volunteers and Partners
Support community engagement and programme delivery.

Core Governance Principles

Transparency

Promoting openness in decision-making, reporting and organisational operations.

Accountability

Taking responsibility for decisions, resources and programme outcomes.

Integrity

Upholding honesty, ethical conduct and responsible leadership.

Inclusiveness

Encouraging meaningful participation and equal opportunities.

Our Commitment

GBL aims to develop appropriate governance policies, maintain accurate organisational records, promote responsible financial management and regularly review its programmes and performance.
