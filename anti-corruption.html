ANTI-CORRUPTION AND INTEGRITY POLICY

Organisation: Green Circular Lab (GBL)

Policy area: Ethics, Accountability and Transparency

Geographical scope: Uganda and all GBL activities

Status: Proposed policy pending formal approval

Important: This is a proposed institutional policy. It becomes an official GBL policy after review and approval by the organisation's authorised governing body.

1. Introduction

Green Circular Lab (GBL) is committed to integrity, accountability, transparency and responsible leadership. The organisation recognises that corruption undermines development, weakens public trust and prevents communities from benefiting fairly from resources and opportunities.

GBL therefore seeks to prevent, identify and respond to corruption in all its programmes, partnerships, procurement processes and organisational activities.

2. Purpose

This policy aims to:

  • Prevent bribery, fraud, theft and misuse of organisational resources.
  • Promote transparency in financial and administrative decisions.
  • Protect organisational funds, property and equipment.
  • Encourage ethical leadership and responsible behaviour.
  • Provide safe ways to report suspected corruption.
  • Strengthen accountability to communities, donors and partners.

3. Scope

This policy applies to GBL leaders, employees, volunteers, interns, consultants, contractors, implementing partners and any person representing the organisation.

4. Zero-Tolerance Commitment

GBL prohibits corruption in all its forms. No individual may offer, request, receive or accept an improper benefit in exchange for influencing an organisational decision or activity.

Prohibited conduct includes:

  • Bribery and improper payments.
  • Fraud, theft and falsification of records.
  • Embezzlement and misuse of funds.
  • Undisclosed conflicts of interest.
  • Manipulation of procurement and recruitment processes.
  • Abuse of authority for personal benefit.
  • Demanding money or favours from beneficiaries.

5. Financial Transparency

GBL shall promote responsible financial management through:

  • Accurate financial records and supporting documentation.
  • Proper authorisation of expenditure.
  • Separation of financial responsibilities where practical.
  • Regular financial reviews and reporting.
  • Independent audits when required and resources permit.
  • Honest reporting to donors and relevant stakeholders.

6. Procurement and Resource Management

Purchasing and contracting decisions should be fair, transparent and based on value for money, quality and organisational needs.

  • Procurement decisions must be properly documented.
  • Personal interests must not influence supplier selection.
  • Staff and representatives must disclose relevant relationships.
  • Organisational property must only be used for authorised purposes.

7. Conflict of Interest

Any person involved in GBL decisions must disclose personal, financial or family interests that could improperly influence their responsibilities.

A person with a conflict should not participate in the relevant decision unless an authorised and documented arrangement permits it.

8. Reporting Misconduct

GBL encourages employees, volunteers, beneficiaries, partners and members of the public to report suspected corruption or misconduct. Reports should be made honestly and in good faith.

9. Whistleblower Protection

GBL shall seek to protect individuals who report concerns in good faith from retaliation, intimidation or unfair treatment. Information will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible, subject to applicable law and fair investigation.

Knowingly false or malicious allegations may be addressed through appropriate organisational procedures.

10. Investigation and Corrective Action

Reported concerns should be assessed fairly, promptly and impartially. Where necessary, GBL may conduct an investigation, preserve relevant records and take appropriate corrective action.

Actions may include internal disciplinary measures, recovery of misused resources, termination of contracts or referral to relevant authorities, in accordance with applicable laws.

11. Responsibilities

  • Leadership: Promote integrity and ensure accountability.
  • Staff and volunteers: Follow this policy and report concerns.
  • Finance personnel: Maintain accurate financial records.
  • Partners and contractors: Observe ethical standards when working with GBL.

12. Policy Review

This policy should be reviewed periodically and updated when necessary to reflect organisational experience, applicable laws and good governance practices.
