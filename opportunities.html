Important Notice

The categories above describe the types of opportunities GBL intends to share. They are not announcements of currently open scholarships, grants or vacancies. Always check the official provider's website for eligibility, application instructions and deadlines before applying.

How to Prepare for Opportunities

  • Prepare an updated CV or personal profile.
  • Keep your academic and professional documents organised.
  • Develop useful digital and communication skills.
  • Research the organisation offering the opportunity.
  • Check eligibility requirements and application deadlines.
  • Never pay an unverified person who promises a scholarship or grant.
