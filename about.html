About Green Circular Lab

Building a greener, smarter and more inclusive future.

1. Who We Are

Green Circular Lab (GBL) is a youth-driven development initiative based in Uganda, committed to building a greener, smarter and more inclusive future.

GBL seeks to address environmental, technological, economic and social challenges by promoting environmental conservation, digital innovation, entrepreneurship and community empowerment.

We believe that young people and communities can become important drivers of sustainable development when equipped with knowledge, practical skills and opportunities.

2. Our Vision

A greener, smarter and more sustainable Africa where communities use innovation, technology and entrepreneurship to create lasting solutions to development challenges.

3. Our Mission

To empower young people and communities through environmental education, circular economy solutions, digital skills, entrepreneurship and inclusive development initiatives.

4. Our Objectives

  • Promote environmental conservation and climate action.
  • Encourage recycling and responsible waste management.
  • Expand access to ICT and digital skills.
  • Promote youth entrepreneurship and innovation.
  • Support community participation in sustainable development.
  • Build partnerships that advance inclusive development.

5. Our Core Values

Integrity

Acting honestly and responsibly in everything we do.

Transparency

Promoting openness and accountability in our work.

Innovation

Encouraging creative solutions to community challenges.

Sustainability

Protecting resources for present and future generations.

Inclusion

Encouraging equal participation and opportunity.

Collaboration

Working together with communities and development partners.

6. Our Areas of Focus

Environment and Climate Action

Environmental awareness, conservation, recycling and circular economy practices.

ICT and Digital Innovation

Digital literacy, technology education and practical innovation.

Youth Entrepreneurship

Business skills, creativity and enterprise development.

Community Empowerment

Inclusive education, participation and sustainable livelihoods.

7. Our Commitment

GBL is committed to integrity, responsible leadership, transparency and meaningful community participation. We aim to develop practical initiatives, build partnerships and contribute to sustainable development in Uganda and Africa.

Our programmes and projects will be developed and implemented progressively as resources, partnerships and organisational capacity become available.
