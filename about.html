Transparency, Accountability and Responsible Financial Management

Our Commitment to Financial Transparency

Green Circular Lab (GBL) is committed to maintaining high standards of financial integrity, accountability and transparency. We recognise that every resource entrusted to our organisation should be managed responsibly and used for its intended purpose.

Our Financial Accountability Principles

  • Accurate financial record keeping.
  • Transparent reporting of income and expenditure.
  • Responsible management of donor contributions.
  • Proper procurement and payment procedures.
  • Internal financial monitoring.
  • Independent external audits when conducted.
  • Compliance with applicable Ugandan laws.
